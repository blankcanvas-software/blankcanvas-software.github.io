1. The service

Vipera is an Android security application that checks APKs in shared storage, reviews user-installed apps, provides threat-intelligence lookups, and offers security guidance. Vipera supports your security decisions; it is not a full-device antivirus engine and does not guarantee that every threat will be detected or blocked.

3. Scanning and security limitations

Vipera's current scan path primarily hashes APKs and checks them against configured reputation providers; its local APK analysis supplies review evidence. It does not inspect every file type, execute apps for behavioral analysis, or guarantee real-time blocking. Vipera may miss new, modified, encrypted, inaccessible, or carefully disguised threats. DNS guidance may not block all ads or trackers. Cloud-based checks depend on network availability and third-party providers.
